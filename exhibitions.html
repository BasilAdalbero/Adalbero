Basil Schu

Bio

Basil Schu is an artist who lives and works in Vienna. He was a member of the Jürgen Kleft artists' group. He organises the Playy Club and is currently collaborating with Laura Dominici. He graduated from the Academy of Fine Arts under Heimo Zobernig, and is also a lecturer at the Academy of Fine Arts.
