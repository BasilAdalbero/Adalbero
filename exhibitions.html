Basil Schu

Bio

Basil Schu is an artist and conceptual designer who lives and works in Vienna. He studied Sculpture at the Academy of Fine Arts Vienna under Heimo Zobernig, and Painting under Erwin Bohatsch followed by Botany (Bachelor) with Jürg Schöneberger and Florian Etl. He completed an Erasmus exchange at the École des Beaux-Arts in Paris, studying under Jean-Marc Bustamante and Guillaume Paris.


He is a founding member of the artist group Jürgen Kleft. He organises the Playy Club, developed in collaboration with Joaneum Graz for the Elevate Festival.


He is currently working in the space between art and conceptual design in collaboration with Laura Dominici. He is a lecturer at the Academy of Fine Arts Vienna.
